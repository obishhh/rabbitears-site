Privacy Policy

Telly! · Last updated 8 October 2026

What we keep

Telly! keeps what it needs to work, and nothing else:

  • Your account: an ID from Sign in with Apple, and the handle you pick. The app asks Apple for nothing but who you are: no name and no email address, and we hold neither. There is no password. So that Apple can be told when you delete your account, the server may keep a token from Apple for your sign-in. It is used for that and nothing else.
  • Your card: the line you write about yourself, your colour, and a photo if you add one. The photo is stored small and in greys.
  • What you write: reviews and the pips on them, rankings of a show's characters, replies, answers to the daily phone-in, calls and your verdicts on other people's, and the applause you give. A review or a reply is kept with the place in the show it was written from.
  • What you rate: your pips for a show or a season.
  • Where you are: how far through each show you have got, and the days you took part, which is what your streak counts.
  • Who you know: who you have added and who has added you, the watch parties you are in or have been asked into, shows friends have put on for you, and anybody you have blocked or reported.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on: your phone's push token, your time zone, and which kinds you have switched off.
  • Sign-in records: when your phone signs in, our authentication provider notes its IP address and the version of the app and system asking.

What stays on your phone

The look you picked, the reviews you have tuned in to and the news you have read are kept on the phone and never sent. Telly! in Messages reads a copy of your place and your recent reviews that the app leaves on the phone; it never signs in. A review you send to a chat travels inside the message itself, through Apple's Messages, and we never see it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics, no crash reporting of our own. The only outside code in the app is the client for our own database.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No browsable directory of people. Searching for someone returns nothing until you have typed at least two characters, so the app can never hand anybody a list of everyone who signed up.
  • No words on your lock screen. A notification says who, which show and where, never anything anybody wrote.

While Telly! is in TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and any feedback you send from TestFlight, and shares them with us under its own terms.

Who can see what

Your handle, your card and what you write are public: anybody using Telly! can read them. Your pips for a show or a season show on your reviews of it; the list of everything you have rated is yours alone.

How far you have got is not public. It is readable by you, and by people in a watch party with you, for that party's show and no other. A friend, meaning somebody you have added who has added you back, can also see the one show you most recently moved on and how far into it you are, and the length of your streak. Who you have added is between you and them.

Your photo's file can be fetched by anybody who has its exact address. The app gives that address only to people who can already see your handle.

Blocking somebody hides what each of you writes from the other, and they are not told.

Those rules are enforced by the database itself through row-level security, not by the app asking nicely. A bug in the app cannot leak where you are.

Where it lives

Accounts, data and photos are stored with Supabase. Notifications are delivered by Apple. Artwork is loaded from The Movie Database's image servers and cast pictures from TVmaze's, which means each can see the IP address of a device asking for a picture. We send them nothing else, and they are never told who you are.

Deleting your account

In the app: Tune, then Delete account. It asks once. Your account goes with everything in it, permanently: your card and photo, what you wrote, your ratings, your progress, your friends and your place in any watch party. A party you started passes to whoever has been in it longest. A report you made about somebody else is kept, without your name on it.

If you can't get into the app, email owenbishop364@gmail.com and say the handle you want removed. To take Telly! out of your Apple account as well, look under Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Telly! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Privacy Policy

Rabbit's Ears · Last updated 20 September 2026

What we collect

Rabbit's Ears stores what it needs to know where you are in a show, and nothing else:

  • Your account: a user ID from Sign in with Apple, and the handle you pick. We never see your name, your password or your real email address. If Apple passes on a private relay address, it is held by our authentication provider and is never used to contact you or given to anyone.
  • What you rate: your ratings, your written reviews, and the position in a season each review was written from.
  • Where you are: how far through a season you have got, for each show you are watching.
  • Your favourites: one show per year, if you pick them.
  • Who you watch with: which simulcasts you are in, and the episode a group has agreed to stop at.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics, no crash reporting. The app contains no third-party SDK that collects anything.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No browsable directory of people. Searching for someone returns nothing until you have typed at least two characters, so the app can never hand anybody a list of everyone who signed up.

Who can see what

Your handle, your ratings and your reviews are public inside the app. How far you have got in a season is not: it is readable by you, and by people in a simulcast with you, and by nobody else.

That rule is enforced by the database itself through row-level security, not by the app asking nicely. A bug in the app cannot leak where you are.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (hosted Postgres). Artwork is loaded from The Movie Database's image servers, which means TMDB can see the IP address of a device asking for a poster. We send them nothing else, and they are never told who you are.

Deleting your account

Email owenbishop364@gmail.com from any address and say the handle you want removed. Your account, your ratings, your reviews, your progress and your simulcast membership go with it, permanently.

Children

Rabbit's Ears is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Rabbit's Ears · A TV rating and review app

Rate a season, write a review, and say how far through you are. Every review remembers the position it was written from, so anything ahead of you stays scrambled until you catch up.

Something's broken, or I have a question

Email owenbishop364@gmail.com. Say what you were doing and what happened. It goes to a person, not a queue.

Why is a review scrambled?

Because it was written from further into the season than you have got. Tap it and the app tells you how many episodes away it is, never what it says. Drag the position slider to where you actually are and anything you have earned comes through on its own.

The app thinks I'm in the wrong place

Open the show and drag the position slider. That one control is the whole truth about where you are, and everything else is worked out from it.

What's the front line?

When you watch something with other people, it's the furthest anyone can talk about without spoiling somebody. It sits wherever the person furthest behind has got to. It isn't a leaderboard, and it never names who's behind.

What's a break?

The episode a group has agreed to stop at. Anyone in the group can move it and anyone can bring somebody in. A break says where everyone meant to stop. It never unlocks a review you haven't earned.

Can I rate individual episodes?

No. The season is the unit, with a slider inside it for where you are. A season takes a week, so being two episodes back is a fact about a calendar, not a score.

Can I change how it looks?

Tune, on channel 4. Picture, Contrast and Tracking change the palette, the weight of the ink and how roomy everything is. Sign out is on the same screen.

Delete my account

Email owenbishop364@gmail.com and say which handle. Everything goes with it, permanently.

A show is missing

Tell us the title. The catalogue doesn't carry talk shows or soaps, and it skips any season over 120 episodes, because a daily strip isn't something you're "up to episode seven of".
